CORE SPECIALIST PRACTICE

End-to-End Tender Management & Bid Support

We support businesses throughout the tender lifecycle, from opportunity screening and Pre-Qualification (PQ) analysis to full bid documentation, portal upload coordination, and post-submission support.

The 6-Stage Tender Lifecycle Sequence

STAGE 01

Discovery & Screening

Multi-portal surveillance across GeM, CPPP, IREPS; capability matching; opportunity executive bulletins.

STAGE 02

Detailed Analysis

NIT/BOQ/GCC/SCC review, 3-year turnover calculation, similar work criteria, EMD/PBG review, disqualification audit.

STAGE 03

Bid Documentation

Compliance matrices, Cover 1 & 2 compilation, declaration drafting, JV/MAF coordination, pre-bid queries.

STAGE 04

Quality Review

Requirement-by-requirement audit, cross-checks against authentic client records, missing document reports.

STAGE 05

Submission Support

Upload coordination on portals, DSC signature verification assistance, token archiving, receipt verification.

STAGE 06

Post-Bid Support

Corrigendum monitoring, technical query clarification replies, record management, LOA checklist verification.

Package Tier Target Requirement Core Scope & Deliverables
Pkg A: Tender Discovery & Screening Businesses seeking relevant opportunities identified and assessed. Daily/weekly portal discovery, capability matching, PQ summaries, direct document links, deadline tracking.
Pkg B: Complete Bid Preparation Support Contractors needing structured bid preparation and submission review. Full NIT/BOQ analysis, compliance matrices, checklists, document compilation, quality review, portal upload coordination.
Pkg C: Dedicated Tender Desk Active contractors handling multiple concurrent public bids. Continuous multi-bid pipeline management, recurring screening, pre-bid query drafting, and regular status reporting.
Integrity & Compliance Protocol: Actual portal submission must be performed by the client or authorized signatory where permitted. Neudact Engineers strictly works with authentic client-provided data and does not fabricate certificates, financial figures, or experience records.
